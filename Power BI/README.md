# Healthcare Performance & Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview
This Power BI project provides a complete view of hospital performance across patient care, operations, experience, and finances.

Key findings show strong overall results for:

- high patient recovery rates 
- balanced gender satisfaction
- solid revenue generation both in volume and financial contribution.

However, opportunities exist to improve waiting times especially in Pediatrics, raise satisfaction in Maternity and Emergency departments, and strengthen performance in lower-performing branches such as Lekki and Ikeja.

The dashboard enables management to monitor trends, identify gaps, and make data driven decisions to enhance patient outcomes and operational efficiency.

The project covers data transformation, data modeling, DAX calculation, visualization, and business insight using Microsoft Power BI.

## Business Problem

Management requires a clear and interactive way to monitor Healthcare Performance and Patient Analytics so as to identify areas that require attention.

The dashboard was designed to answer question such as:

1. What age group represents the largest number of patients?
2. Which states have the highest patient volume?
3. What diagnoses are most common?
4. How many patients are returning?
5. What are the most common patient outcomes?
6. Which department receives the most patients?
7. Which branch handles the most visits?
8. Which departments have lower satisfaction score?
9. Which state generate the most revenue?
10. Which services generate the most revenue?
11. Are state meeting their revenue targets?
12. Which areas require further financial investigation?
13. Which department has the highest satisfaction?
14. Which branch has the lowest satisfaction?
15. Which patient outcomes occur most frequently?
16. What is the relationship between waiting time and satisfaction?
